IPP Hidden Portfolio Risk - preliminary analysis notes

Reporting date: 30 September 2026, parsed from user-provided RupeeVest exports.
Analysis script: python_code/02_portfolio_concentration_analysis.py

Outputs:
- holding_concentration_summary.csv: equity record counts, top-five and top-ten sums.
- sector_exposure_provisional.csv: sector totals based on the working, not-yet-verified map. DO NOT use for final conclusions yet.
- largest_holding_scenarios.csv: hypothetical direct impacts for 10% and 20% falls in each scheme's largest equity holding.
- top_five_concentration.png: chart of top-five concentration sums.

Important interpretation notes:
1. Figures are preliminary calculations from the supplied RupeeVest exports, not independently verified AMC disclosures.
2. Top-five and top-ten values are sums of reported equity holding weights as percentages of scheme NAV. They are not normalised to equity-only weight.
3. The five schemes have different investment mandates, including a hybrid fund and an index fund; comparisons require this caveat.
4. Sector mappings are provisional. Do not report sector rankings or diversification-gap conclusions as final until each company is verified against official company-level classification data.
5. No missing values were imputed and no portfolio weights were automatically normalised.
6. Scenario calculations are simple sensitivity estimates, not forecasts or investment advice.
